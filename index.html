Our story

Calanthia began on a kitchen table with a pot of soy wax and a stubborn idea: a candle should make a room feel slower. Every one is poured by hand in small batches and left to cure for two weeks, so the scent fills the room.

  • 100% soy wax
  • Essential oils
  • Cotton wicks
  • Reusable jars
